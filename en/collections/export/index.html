Global textile supply

From fibre to fabric — supplied to the world

Hieron is an international solutions partner supplying woven fabrics, yarns and filaments to textile manufacturers around the world. We serve a wide spectrum of production — from workwear and home textiles to fashion, technical and promotional textiles — providing our customers with the raw materials they need, exactly to the technical and quality specifications they require.

Our fabric portfolio covers plain, twill, panama, half-panama, ripstop and microfiber qualities — the fundamental constructions of modern textile production. Depending on the end use and production process, our products can be supplied greige, dyed or printed.

Our yarn and filament range consists of 100% cotton, 100% polyester, cotton-polyester blends and filament yarns, offered in dyed or pre-dyed (greige) options according to our customers' production requirements.

At Hieron, we see every order not merely as a sale, but as the beginning of a long-term collaboration. With a reliable supply network, flexible order quantities and a customer-focused approach, we aim to build sustainable partnerships that create value at every stage of the textile supply chain.

We supply the right raw material for your textile production — in the right quality, at the right time.
